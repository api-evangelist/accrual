# Accrual

> Accrual is an augmented accounting intelligence platform for accounting firms and growing service businesses — a complete accounting workspace spanning engagement management, document intake, data processing, tax return generation, assurance workflows, and client collaboration. Backed by General Catalyst. US-hosted, SOC 2 Type II attested. No public API or developer program is published at this time.
